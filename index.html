About this Project

How it started

Hi, I'm a high school student who likes chemistry. I never planned to make this website, but I've always liked explaining chemistry concepts and making tutorials for my friends. Eventually, I just thought it might be fun to try building chemistry related.

Stoichio originally started as a simple chemistry calculator. Everything in the Tools section is what this entire website used to be.

After showing it to some friends and asking for feedback, a few of them suggested adding lessons, notes, and practice questions, and that's how the rest of Stoichio came about.

Stoichio started as something I made for myself and my friends, but I hope it can eventually help students beyond just the people I know. Whether you're trying to understand a topic, need some practice, or just want to check a calculation, I hope there's something here that can help you.

Right now, Stoichio only covers some of the more basic chemistry topics, but I'm hoping to expand it and add more advanced content over time.

I'm still a student myself and this is just a little side project, so there might be bugs, mistakes or things that don't work properly. I'll keep adding and fixing things as I go.

FAQ

What arrow can I use?

Any of these work: ->, =, →, ⟶, ⇒. If the site can't find an arrow, it tells you what it saw and gives you an example.

Why does the answer show coefficients like "5 O2"?

That is the balanced form. It means five molecules of O2. When the coefficient is 1, the site leaves it out, so you see H2O instead of 1 H2O.

How do I write subscripts?

As plain numbers right after the element. H2O, not H₂O. Fe2(SO4)3, not Fe₂(SO₄)₃. Parentheses and brackets both work.
